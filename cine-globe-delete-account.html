Cine Globe · Account help

Request account deletion

You can ask Cine Globe support to review a request to delete your account. The current app does not have an in-app account-deletion control or an automatic deletion API, so sending an email does not itself delete your account or its records.

How to submit a request

  1. Email support@bookmywebsite.in with the subject Delete My Cine Globe Account.
  2. Include the email address or phone number associated with your Cine Globe account and your sign-in method (email, phone, or Google). Do not send your password, OTP, or payment-card details.
  3. Support may ask for reasonable information to verify that you control the account. Wait for support to confirm the request and explain what can be removed and any applicable timeline.
Email support

Important: the current app implementation does not specify a deletion service-level deadline and does not automatically remove Firebase or payment records when you contact support. Do not assume deletion is complete until support confirms it.

Information to discuss with support

Depending on your account and features used, records associated with your account may include:

  • Firebase Authentication account identifiers and account profile details.
  • Firestore user/profile records and profile settings.
  • Saved titles, watch history, playback progress, ratings, and watch-session records.
  • Push-notification token and associated platform/device registration information.
  • Subscription status, payment order identifiers, and related transaction records.

These records are held in different app and provider systems. The app does not provide a self-service process that deletes all of them together. Support must confirm the scope and outcome for your request; this page does not claim that every record is automatically or immediately erased.

Data stored on your device

Some profiles, preferences, library data, and downloaded video files are stored locally on your device. A support request cannot remotely remove local files. Before uninstalling, remove downloads and other locally stored items using the app or your device's app-storage controls. Removing the app does not by itself delete account records stored by Cine Globe or its providers.

Records that may need to be retained

Payment/order and subscription records may exist in Cine Globe's Firebase backend and in Razorpay systems. Some records may need to be kept for applicable legal or accounting duties, payment disputes, fraud prevention, or security. Provider retention is controlled by the relevant provider. The current app code and policy do not define a fixed retention period; ask support to explain what applies to your request.

More information

Read the Cine Globe Privacy Policy for the information and services used by the app. For account deletion questions, contact support@bookmywebsite.in.
