Delete Your Cine Globe Account

If you no longer wish to use your Cine Globe account, you can request its permanent deletion at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to support@kannadaott.in.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete My Account.
  3. Include the email address or phone number associated with your Cine Globe account.

What Happens After Your Request

After verifying your request, we will permanently delete your account and associated personal information within 30 days.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Your account profile
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Watch history
  • Favorites and preferences
  • Authentication information associated with your account

Data That May Be Retained

Some information may be retained where required by applicable law, for fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or other legal obligations.
