Cine Globe Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 29, 2026

Cine Globe ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Cine Globe mobile application, developed and operated by Fuelo Technologies.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Cine Globe, we may collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Google Account Information (if you sign in using Google)
  • User Profile Information
  • Subscription Status
  • App Usage Information
  • Device Information required for app functionality

2. How We Collect Information

We collect information when you:

  • Create an account
  • Sign in using Email & Password
  • Sign in using Phone OTP
  • Sign in with Google
  • Purchase a subscription (if available)
  • Use our streaming services

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate your login
  • Provide access to streaming content
  • Maintain your watch history and preferences
  • Improve app performance
  • Provide customer support
  • Protect against fraud and misuse

4. Authentication

Cine Globe supports the following login methods:

  • Email and Password
  • Phone Number OTP Verification
  • Google Sign-In

Authentication is securely managed using trusted authentication services.

5. Video Streaming

The app streams licensed video content over secure internet connections. Your viewing activity may be stored to provide features such as Continue Watching, Watch History, and Favorites.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We use trusted third-party service providers to operate the application. These providers may process personal information on our behalf only as necessary to provide their services, such as:

  • Google Firebase Authentication
  • Google Sign-In
  • Cloud database services used to operate the app
  • Google Play Billing (if subscriptions are offered)

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and security measures to protect your personal information. Data is transmitted using encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. Data Retention

Your information is retained only as long as necessary to provide our services or comply with legal obligations.

9. Children's Privacy

Cine Globe is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your Rights

You may request to:

  • Access your information
  • Update your account information
  • Delete your account
  • Delete your personal information

11. Delete Account Request

Users can request deletion of their Cine Globe account by emailing support@kannadaott.in with the subject "Delete My Account."

Once we verify your request, your account and associated personal information will be permanently deleted within 30 days unless we are required by law to retain certain information.

12. Third-Party Services

The application may use trusted third-party services including:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Google Sign-In
  • Google Play Billing (if applicable)

These services have their own privacy policies governing their handling of information.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page along with the updated date.

14. TMDB Attribution

Cine Globe uses The Movie Database (TMDB) API to display movie and television information. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
